04How I work

A clear path
to launch day

No black boxes. You get a working build on your phone every week.

  1. Step 01

    Discover

    We pin down the users, the core flows and what version one really needs.

  2. Step 02

    Prototype

    Clickable flows and a component system, so we test the feel before we write the code.

  3. Step 03

    Build

    Weekly builds on TestFlight and Play internal testing, so you see progress on your own phone.

  4. Step 04

    Launch

    Store listings, review and release, then analytics and iteration once real users arrive.
